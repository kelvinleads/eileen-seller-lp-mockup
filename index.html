What I check before I list any home

Before I list, I go through 4 checks using actual past sales records. They tell me who can buy your home, and what price they will accept.

On the free call, you'll get

  • Why your home hasn't sold yet
  • What price makes sense, based on recent sales in your block
  • Who is most likely to buy your home, and how to reach them
  • What to do next, even if you don't list with me
  1. F1

    Frequency: how often units sell

    If only a handful sold in your block, waiting for buyers to find you won't work. I go out and find them.

  2. F2

    Footprint: size of your estate

    It decides which past sales buyers compare you to, so I price and present your unit to stand out from the ones around it.

  3. F3

    Freshness: age of your home

    Once a flat is around 30 years old, banks lend less and some buyers can't use as much CPF. I market to buyers who can actually pay.

  4. F4

    Footing: number of past owners

    Buyers look up what the last owner paid. I set a price you can explain, so the offers that come in are serious.
